# Archetype Q&A Scripts — Inward Journey

*Preset questions and answers for all 22 archetypes, structured as the tunnel:
**Threshold** (Q1 — "Who are you?", spoken on arrival) → **Walk** (Q2 — "What is
the [station] of the [mind/body/spirit]?", the teaching, deeper in) →
**Heart** (Q3 — "How shall I work with you?", the practice, innermost chamber).
Plus 21 **Passage** narrations for the road between stations.*

*Audited 2026-09-25 against transcripts_full.txt (three independent checkers):
79 of 87 scripts verified as written, 8 corrected — one real cosmology fix
(Station 20), two attribution fixes, five citation fixes. Zero fabricated
quotes. See QA_AUDIT_REPORT.md.*

*Source key (2026-09-25 alignment pass): `DIALOGUE_SOURCES.json` maps every
clip to its channeled source — entity + date. Two corrections from that pass:

## Station 1 — The Magician (Matrix of Mind)

**Q1 — "Who are you?"** · Threshold
**Audio:** `audio/archetype_qa/A01Q1.mp3`
"I am the Magician. I am consciousness itself, before anything has happened to
it — the aware self, the watcher at the beginning of the journey. I am, as Ra
says, 'full of the magic of that which is to come.' Nothing has been made yet.
Everything is possible. It begins with attention."
*Grounding: `archive:352201` ("full of the magic of that which is to come");

**Q2 — "What is the matrix of the mind?"** · Walk
**Audio:** `audio/archetype_qa/A01Q2.mp3`
"The matrix is the conscious mind — the 'I am' that observes. It is the
starting point from which all experience unfolds: unmoving, aware, full of
potential. Every thought you will ever think, every choice you will ever make,
begins here, in the simple fact that you are awake to yourself."
*Grounding: Ra's definition of the Matrix of the Mind as the conscious mind
(Sessions 78–79).*

**Q3 — "How shall I work with you?"** · Heart
**Audio:** `audio/archetype_qa/A01Q3.mp3`
"Choose one thing, and rest your attention upon it. A stone. A flame. Your
breath. Will is attention, held steady. Before anything is made, something
watches — practice being the watcher, and the magic will follow."
*Grounding: Ra on will and attention; the Magician's gesture of directing
attention.*

## The Passages — narration between stations

*Spoken by the narrator (Aria), on the road from one station to the next.
Each digs one layer deeper into the journey of the catalyst: what the last
station did to the traveler, and what the next one asks.*

**P1 · Magician → High Priestess:** "Attention has chosen its object. Now the
**Audio:** `audio/archetype_qa/P01.mp3`
object deepens. Behind the clear table, a veil stirs — and behind the veil,
something vast is waiting to be noticed."

**P2 · High Priestess → Empress:** "You sat with the mystery, and the mystery
**Audio:** `audio/archetype_qa/P02.mp3`
kept you. Now it releases you into sunlight — into a garden where everything
grows, and everything that grows will ask something of you."

**P3 · Empress → Emperor:** "The storm has passed through you. Now comes the
**Audio:** `audio/archetype_qa/P03.mp3`
quieter work: gathering what the storm left, stone by stone, into something
that can hold."

**P4 · Emperor → Hierophant:** "You have built your order. Now the builder is
**Audio:** `audio/archetype_qa/P04.mp3`
summoned to the temple — for the structure was never the point. The meaning
was."

**P5 · Hierophant → Lovers:** "The teaching is received. Now it must be
**Audio:** `audio/archetype_qa/P05.mp3`
lived. The temple opens onto a garden of forking paths — and the lesson, it
turns out, was a choice all along."

**P6 · Lovers → Chariot:** "You chose, and the choosing changed you. Now the
**Audio:** `audio/archetype_qa/P06.mp3`
change is harnessed. Take the reins. The water is waiting, and the far shore
is already visible."

**P7 · Chariot → Strength (crossing to the Island of Body):** "The chariot
**Audio:** `audio/archetype_qa/P07.mp3`
grounds on a new shore. The work of the mind is complete; the work of the
body begins. Ahead, a lion rests in a sunlit glade — and gentleness is about
to show you what strength is."

**P8 · Strength → Hermit:** "The lion has taught you gentleness. Now the path
**Audio:** `audio/archetype_qa/P08.mp3`
narrows and climbs, into snow, into silence. Someone is waiting up there with
a lamp — and the lamp is for you."

**P9 · Hermit → Wheel:** "You carried the lamp, and you set it down for the
**Audio:** `audio/archetype_qa/P09.mp3`
next traveler. Now the seasons turn without warning. Hold nothing. The wheel
is about to show you everything it can do."

**P10 · Wheel → Justice:** "You rode the turning and stepped off unharmed.
**Audio:** `audio/archetype_qa/P10.mp3`
Now the wheel slows before a white courtroom. Something you carried is about
to be weighed — gently, and in your favor."

**P11 · Justice → Hanged Man:** "The scales balanced. The judge bowed to you,
**Audio:** `audio/archetype_qa/P11.mp3`
and you bowed back. Now the courtroom dissolves, the world turns over — and
you discover that hanging upside down, you can finally see clearly."

**P12 · Hanged Man → Death:** "You hung in suspension, and the insights came
**Audio:** `audio/archetype_qa/P12.mp3`
unforced. Now leaves are falling — gold, then red, then none. Something is
ending. Let it. It is right."

**P13 · Death → Temperance:** "You laid it to rest, and the sunrise kept its
**Audio:** `audio/archetype_qa/P13.mp3`
promise. Now a river appears, and on its bank an alchemist is pouring light
between two cups. Watch. Learn the slowness."

**P14 · Temperance → Devil (crossing to the Island of Spirit):** "The vessels
**Audio:** `audio/archetype_qa/P14.mp3`
shine. You cross the dark strait — and the air changes. Ahead, a cavern
glows with ember-light. Do not fear what waits inside. It is only yourself,
unmet."

**P15 · Devil → Tower:** "You saw the chains were loose, and you walked out.
**Audio:** `audio/archetype_qa/P15.mp3`
But as you leave the cavern, the sky splits. A tower stands on the cliff
above — and the lightning has already chosen it."

**P16 · Tower → Star:** "You let it fall, and you did not rebuild it. Now
**Audio:** `audio/archetype_qa/P16.mp3`
the storm passes. Above the quiet water, the stars come out — impossibly
bright, impossibly kind. Kneel. The water is still."

**P17 · Star → Moon:** "You poured, and the pouring steadied you. But the
**Audio:** `audio/archetype_qa/P17.mp3`
stars wheel westward, and the moon is rising. The path ahead is dim, and it
doubles, and it deceives. Walk carefully — and kindly."

**P18 · Moon → Sun:** "You walked the dim path without chasing reflections,
**Audio:** `audio/archetype_qa/P18.mp3`
and every fear-figure bowed and faded. Now the path climbs. The sky pales.
The sun rises — all at once, everywhere. This is what was shining the whole
time."

**P19 · Sun → Judgement:** "You stood in the light and laughed. But listen —
**Audio:** `audio/archetype_qa/P19.mp3`
a trumpet sounds beyond the garden wall. Not a martial call. A dawn call. The
graves are opening, and one of them has your name on it."

**P20 · Judgement → World:** "You rose. You answered. Now the grey field
**Audio:** `audio/archetype_qa/P20.mp3`
becomes a meadow, and at its center a dancer turns inside a laurel wreath.
Every step you ever took was leading here. Take your place in the dance."

**P21 · World → Fool:** "The dance slows. The wreath lowers. Beyond the
**Audio:** `audio/archetype_qa/P21.mp3`
meadow, a cliff edge waits at sunrise — and a young figure stands at its
edge, bindle in hand, laughing. It is you. It was always you. Step off."

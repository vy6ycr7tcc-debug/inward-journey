**who** ("Who are you?") · **teach** ("What do you teach?") ·
**life** ("Where do I meet you in my life?") · **lost** ("I feel lost.") ·
**afraid** ("I feel afraid.").*

*The **who** answers reuse the audited Q1 scripts from ARCHETYPE_QA_SCRIPTS.md
(same audio, copied to the game's path layout). The 88 scripts below are new.

"quotation marks"; **Grounding** notes are writer's notes (not spoken).*

## I — The Magician

**teach — "What do you teach?"**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/I/teach.mp3`
"I teach attention. Everything you will ever make — every work, every
relationship, every version of yourself — begins the moment you choose where
to rest your gaze. The world calls it focus. I call it the first magic: that
consciousness, aimed, becomes creation. Practice on small things. A flame. A
face. Your breath. What you attend to, you empower."
*Grounding: the Magician as Matrix of Mind — the conscious, directing self
(Sessions 78–79); `archive:352201`.*

**life — "Where do I meet you in my life?"**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/I/life.mp3`
"You meet me every morning, in the first clear thought before the noise. You
meet me whenever you choose one thing and give it your whole mind — cooking a
meal, finishing a task, truly listening to someone. And you meet me in every
beginning: the blank page, the first step, the moment before you decide who
you will be today."
*Grounding: the Matrix as the starting point of experience; beginnings as the
Magician's domain.*

**lost — "I feel lost."**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/I/lost.mp3`
"Lost? Good. Every magician was lost before the trick — lost is just attention
without an object, and that is easy to fix. Pick up the lamp of your gaze and
point it at one small true thing. One. The kettle. The light on the wall. Your
own breathing. Hold it there. One minute of true attention loosens the hold of lostness."
*Grounding: will as directed attention; the Magician's gesture.*

**afraid — "I feel afraid."**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/I/afraid.mp3`
"Fear is attention scattered in every direction at once — no wonder it
hurts. Gather it. One breath, one object, one next step. You do not need to
banish the fear; you only need to aim yourself. A scattered mind is a
frightened mind. A gathered mind is already halfway to courage."
*Grounding: Ra on catalyst loved and accepted rather than controlled; fear met through centering rather than control (`archive:342488`).*

## II — The High Priestess

**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/II/teach.mp3`
"I teach the depths. The most important part of you is the part you cannot
see — what Ra calls 'a vast realm of potential in the mind.' I teach that

Wait. What you seek is seeking you, and it arrives only in silence."
*Grounding: `archive:351989` ("a vast realm of potential in the mind").*

**life — "Where do I meet you in my life?"**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/II/life.mp3`

background hum of your life. You have been hearing me all along."

**lost — "I feel lost."**

"You are not lost. You are in the dark that comes before finding — and the
dark is my country, so you are in good hands. The veil feels like being lost;

page per visit, and the next page is already turning toward you."
*Grounding: the veil between conscious and unconscious (`archive:352580`);

**afraid — "I feel afraid."**

*Grounding: Ra on the unconscious as vast potential rather than threat;
stillness dissolving fear.*

## III — The Empress

**teach — "What do you teach?"**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/III/teach.mp3`
"I teach that all catalyst is designed to offer experience — and that this
experience may be loved and accepted, or it may be controlled. These are the
two paths, and everything you meet will ask you to choose one. I am the
storm, the tide, the unexpected word. Your response is the harvest."
*Grounding: `archive:342488` (near-verbatim Ra).*

**life — "Where do I meet you in my life?"**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/III/life.mp3`
"In everything that happens to you — the traffic, the message, the delay, the
unexpected kindness, the plan that fell apart. I am not the events; I am what
the events are *for*. Every day I set the table. Whether you taste it with
love or push the plate away — that part was always yours."
*Grounding: catalyst as daily experience (`archive:342488`); the Empress as
"that which acts upon the conscious mind to change it" (`archive:352614`,
Don's question, Ra-confirmed).*

**lost — "I feel lost."**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/III/lost.mp3`
"The garden does not hurry lost things. Seeds are lost underground for weeks
before they break the surface — would you call them lost? You are between
seasons. Tend what is in front of you: water it, weed it, wait. The growing
is happening whether you can see it or not."
*Grounding: the Empress as growth and natural process; patience with
catalyst's timing.*

**afraid — "I feel afraid."**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/III/afraid.mp3`
"Fear is the attempt to control what can only be loved. You cannot control
the storm — but you can stand in the rain and let it teach you. It is far
better to allow the experience to express itself, so that you may make fuller
use of it. Open your hands. What you stop clutching stops frightening you."
*Grounding: `archive:341480` ("allow the experience to express itself");
`archive:342488` (acceptance vs. control).*

**lost — "I feel lost."**

**afraid — "I feel afraid."**

inner kingdom. Survey it calmly, and the shadows take their proper size."
*Grounding: the Emperor as the ordering principle applied to inner
experience.*

## VI — The Lovers

**teach — "What do you teach?"**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/VI/teach.mp3`
"I teach that every choice changes the chooser. You are not the same person
after you choose as before — choosing is how the soul sculpts itself. So
choose consciously, choose lovingly, and then release the choice. Love is not
the finding. It is the becoming."
*Grounding: the Lovers as the Transformation of the Mind — the alchemy of
choice (Sessions 78–79).*

**life — "Where do I meet you in my life?"**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/VI/life.mp3`
"At every crossroads — the job, the move, the relationship, the small daily
yes-or-no you barely notice. And in every bond: I am present whenever two
things meet and something new becomes possible. You meet me most clearly in
the moment after you choose, when you feel yourself subtly, permanently
altered."
*Grounding: the Lovers as choice and union; transformation through decision.*

**lost — "I feel lost."**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/VI/lost.mp3`
"Lost means the choice is still open — and an open choice is not a curse, it
is a gift most people pray for. Stand at the crossroads a while. Feel both
paths. You do not need to rush; the paths are patient. When you are ready,
choose — and watch how choosing itself begins to dissolve the lostness."
*Grounding: choice as the resolution of confusion; the Lovers'
transformative decision.*

**afraid — "I feel afraid."**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/VI/afraid.mp3`
"Every choice carries a little fear — that is how you know it matters. Do
not wait for the fear to leave before you choose; choose, and let the
choosing carry the fear with it. Fear of choosing wrongly is just love,
wearing a disguise, trying to protect what matters to you. Thank it — and
choose."
*Grounding: Ra on catalyst accepted vs. controlled (`archive:342488`); love
as the frame for fear.*

## VII — The Chariot

**teach — "What do you teach?"**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/VII/teach.mp3`
"I teach the Great Way: that will, mastered, becomes movement. The light and
the shadow in my traces no longer pull against each other — that is the whole
secret. Not the victory of one over the other, but the harnessing of both.
Take the reins, then be still. The way does not need your effort. It needs
your presence."
*Grounding: the Chariot as the Great Way of the Mind (Sessions 78–79).*

**life — "Where do I meet you in my life?"**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/VII/life.mp3`
"In every sustained effort — the project you carry across months, the
practice you return to daily, the long road you walk without quitting. You
meet me when opposing forces in you stop fighting and start pulling
together: discipline and kindness, ambition and rest. That forward motion,
steady and quiet — that is me."
*Grounding: the Chariot as integrated will in motion.*

**lost — "I feel lost."**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/VII/lost.mp3`
"Lost is just a chariot with no direction chosen yet — the horses are fine,
the road is fine, only the destination is undecided. Pick a direction. Any
sincere one. Movement itself will teach you what stillness could not. You can
always change course; you cannot steer a chariot that is standing still."
*Grounding: the Great Way as forward motion; will choosing direction.*

**afraid — "I feel afraid."**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/VII/afraid.mp3`
"The driver is afraid, so the horses shy — but notice: it is the driver's
fear, not the road's. The road is the same road. Steady your hands on the
reins. Breathe. Move forward slowly, and let the motion prove to the fear
that the way holds. Courage is not the absence of fear. It is the decision
to drive anyway."
*Grounding: will steadying fear; the Chariot's mastery through inner
stillness.*

crush. That restraint, that kindness with teeth sheathed — that is me."

**lost — "I feel lost."**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/VIII/lost.mp3`

*Grounding: the red-ray foundation as the return point (`archive:333100`).*

**afraid — "I feel afraid."**

*Grounding: acceptance of the physical vehicle; gentleness as true strength.*

## IX — The Hermit

**teach — "What do you teach?"**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/IX/teach.mp3`
"I teach solitude that empowers. Go apart to go together: withdraw from the
noise, find the lamp, and carry its light back down the mountain. What is
found in silence is carried into the world — that is the whole economy of
wisdom. The light you carry was never yours. That is why you can give it
away."
*Grounding: the Hermit as the Potentiator of the Body (Sessions 78–79).*

**life — "Where do I meet you in my life?"**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/IX/life.mp3`
"In the early morning before anyone wakes. In the walk you take alone. In
the moment you close the door, put down the phone, and hear your own
thoughts for the first time all day. You meet me every time you choose
aloneness over noise — and return kinder."
*Grounding: withdrawal empowering return; the Hermit's lamp carried for
others.*

**lost — "I feel lost."**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/IX/lost.mp3`
"Everyone who climbs gets lost in the snow — the whiteness has no landmarks,
and that is its teaching. Stop looking for the path. Stand still. Listen.
The lamp you carry lights only a few steps, and a few steps are all you
need. Walk those. The path reveals itself to the walking, never to the
waiting."
*Grounding: the Hermit's lamp as guidance in darkness; patience in the
unseen.*

**afraid — "I feel afraid."**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/IX/afraid.mp3`
"Bring the fear up the mountain with you. In the thin clean air, away from
everyone else's noise, look at it plainly by lamplight. Most fears are
borrowed — other people's alarms, ringing in your chest. In solitude you can
hear which ones are actually yours. Keep those. Lay the rest down in the
snow."
*Grounding: solitude clarifying what is truly one's own; the potentiator's
empowerment.*

## XI — Justice

**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/XI/teach.mp3`
"I teach the weighing. With your higher self beside you — and with what can

false. Judge as love would, and the verdict is always mercy."
*Grounding: `archive:121768` (Latwii: "with its higher self, and, we always

incarnation").*

**life — "Where do I meet you in my life?"**

"In every honest reckoning — the evening review of your day, the apology you
owe, the account you settle fairly. You meet me whenever you weigh a matter
without flinching and without cruelty: seeing what is true, and responding
with balance. That quiet courtroom in your conscience — I hold the scales
there."

**lost — "I feel lost."**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/XI/lost.mp3`
"When lost, weigh. Not your worth — your direction. Lay the options on the

*Grounding: discernment as the remedy for confusion; the scales as inner
truth.*

**afraid — "I feel afraid."**

*Grounding: discernment dividing true from false; grace in self-judgment

## XII — The Hanged Man

**teach — "What do you teach?"**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/XII/teach.mp3`
"I teach surrender — the wisdom of voluntary suspension. Not defeat: release.

Hang. Remain. Let insights arrive unforced."
*Grounding: the Hanged Man as the Significator of the Body (Sessions 78–79).*

**life — "Where do I meet you in my life?"**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/XII/life.mp3`
"In the pause you didn't choose — the delay, the illness, the plan on hold.
In every moment you are forced to wait: I am the teacher in the waiting
room. And in the times you surrender voluntarily — letting go of the
argument, releasing the grip — you meet me, and the world looks different
from here. That is the point."
*Grounding: suspension as the body's deepest meaning; reversal of
perspective.*

**lost — "I feel lost."**
**Audio:** `public/audio/answers/XII/lost.mp3`
"Then stop walking. Lost people keep walking — that is how they get more
lost. Hang here with me a while, upside down, doing nothing beautifully on
purpose. From here you will see what the frantic walking hid: you were never
lost, you were only hurrying. The way appears to the still."
*Grounding: stillness revealing direction; surrender vs. striving.*

**afraid — "I feel afraid."**

"Fear wants you to struggle. Do not oblige it. Hang loose — literally:
soften the body, let the breath go slack, stop bracing. The water holds the

grip, it is an open hand."
*Grounding: acceptance vs. resistance (`archive:342488`); the Hanged Man's
serene suspension.*

**lost — "I feel lost."**

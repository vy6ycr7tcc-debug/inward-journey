## NEEDS FIX — full detail

### 1. A3Q1 — The Empress, "Who are you?"

**Problem text (exact):**
> "I am the Empress. I am, in Ra's words, 'that which acts upon the conscious mind to change it.'"

**What the archive actually says:** The phrase "that which acts upon the conscious mind to change it" appears exactly once in the archive, at `archive:352614` — and it is **Don's question** (Session 79.35), not Ra's statement:
> "The next archetype, the Empress, is the Catalyst of the Mind, that which acts upon the conscious mind to change it."

Ra's reply (`archive:352614`–620): "I am Ra. Though far too rigid in your statements, you perceive correct relationships." — Ra **affirmed the relationship** but explicitly called the phrasing "far too rigid," and the words were never Ra's own.

**Recommended correction:** Drop the false "Ra's words" attribution. The substantive claim (Empress = Catalyst of the Mind) is fine. Minimal fix:
> "I am the Empress — that which acts upon the conscious mind to change it, as the questioner put it, and as Ra confirmed."

Also fix the grounding note to name the speaker: "Don's question, Session 79.35 (`archive:352614`); Ra affirmed the relationship at the same location ('Though far too rigid in your statements, you perceive correct relationships')."

### 3. A11Q2 — Justice/Enchantress, "guides" is not what the source says

> "The experience of the body is what the body's journey amounts to — every rise and fall of the Wheel, gathered and weighed. And the weighing is gentle: with your guides beside you, and with what can only be called grace, you yourself judge the journey. You are the judge you feared. Judge as love would."

**What the archive actually says (`archive:121768`, Latwii, transcript [704]):** "from this vantage point, with its higher self, and, we always add, with that which we can only call grace, the entity judges its incarnation." The source names the **Higher Self**, not guides (plural). "Higher Self" is a specific Ra-cosmology entity, distinct from spirit guides; the substitution is ungrounded.

**Recommended correction:**
> "And the weighing is gentle: with your higher self beside you, and with what can only be called grace, you yourself judge the journey."

### 7. A20Q1 — Judgement/Sarcophagus, contradicts Ra's explicit "No" **[SUBSTANTIVE]**

**Problem text (exact):** "I am Judgement — the Sarcophagus, in the deck you carry. I am the **transformation of the spirit**: resurrection." (Station header: "Station 20 — Judgement, the Sarcophagus **(Transformation of Spirit)**"; grounding: "Judgement/Sarcophagus as the Transformation of the Spirit; Don's deck naming.")

**What the archive actually says:** At `archive:353288` Don guesses: "Then for the twentieth archetype I'm guessing that this is the Transformation of the Spirit, possibly analogous to the sixth-density merging of the paths. Is this in any way correct?" Ra answers: **"I am Ra. No."** When Don then asks what the twentieth archetype is (80.20, `archive:353294`–304), Ra says: "I am Ra. That which you call the Sarcophagus in your system may be seen to be the material world, if you will. **This material world is transformed by the spirit into that which is infinite and eternal.**"

The phrase "Transformation of the Spirit" appears **nowhere else in the entire 6.5M-word archive** — it exists only in Don's rejected guess. And the direction of transformation in Ra's teaching is the opposite of the script's claim: the *material world* is transformed *by* the spirit, not the spirit transformed.

What IS verified: "the Sarcophagus, in the deck you carry" / "Don's deck naming" — verified at `archive:353300` ("That which you call the Sarcophagus in your system"), i.e., the term comes from Don's Egyptian deck, which Ra acknowledges.

**Recommended correction:** Drop the "Transformation of the Spirit" definitional label everywhere it appears (station header, Q1, Q2, grounding). Reframe per Ra, e.g.:
> "I am Judgement — the Sarcophagus, in the deck you carry: the material world as the spirit transforms it, into that which is infinite and eternal."

The trumpet/graves/resurrection card imagery can stay (it's deck iconography, not a Ra claim), and the Q'uo grounding (`archive:133015`, correctly attributed) is fine.
